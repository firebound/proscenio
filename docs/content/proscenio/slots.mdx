## Active Slot

A slot can follow a bone two ways, and both export the same and rebuild identically in Godot.

**Bind to Bone** is the safe route: it keeps the Empty object-parented (so the flat attachment quads stay in the picture plane) and adds a `Child Of` constraint whose inverse cancels the bone rest, so the slot rides only the bone's pose delta. It stays flat for any bone orientation.

**Hand bone-parenting** the Empty also exports, but only for bones pointing into the screen; an in-plane bone tilts the flat quads edge-on and collapses them. The Active Slot subpanel shows how each slot follows (a Proscenio constraint or a bone parent), names its parent, and warns when a bone-parented slot's bone lies in the picture plane. **Unbind** stops the follow, removing whichever relation is live. Binding refuses when a slot already follows, so rebinding after a move is Unbind then Bind.
